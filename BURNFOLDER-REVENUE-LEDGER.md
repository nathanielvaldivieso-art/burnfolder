## What this ledger does

Add one row whenever money actually clears. This distinguishes revenue from attention and shows which parts of the practice can repeat.

- **Gross:** Total amount the buyer/client paid
- **Fees:** Stripe, Bandcamp, marketplace, payment-processing, or platform fees
- **Direct cost:** Materials, shipping subsidy, hired labor, venue cost, or other cost caused by that sale
- **Tax reserve:** Money set aside for taxes; this is not a final tax calculation
- **Net contribution:** Gross minus fees, direct costs, and tax reserve
- **Hours:** Time required to make, sell, administer, and fulfill the offer
- **Source:** How the person found or reached Burnfolder
- **Supporter:** New or repeat buyer/client
- **Permission:** Whether they explicitly agreed to future contact
