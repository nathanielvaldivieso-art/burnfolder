# PHOTONEGATIVE — Pitch leads

Tabled until the press window. Use this when there is a finished release to pitch. No paid spam services; only human, fit-first outreach.

Handles and site links live in [`socials/README.md`](socials/README.md).

## How to find more leads

1. **Listen first.** Find 3–5 blogs or playlists that already cover music close to yours. Read their about/submit pages for tone and rules.
2. **Hype Machine / Elbo.ws** — search for artists with a similar slow-build, ambient, or indie-experimental sound and note which blogs covered them.
3. **Spotify playlist search** — type a mood/genre keyword, open playlists, read curator names, look for a “submit” link in the playlist description.
4. **SubmitHub / Groover** — only if you want paid feedback on fit. Do not buy bulk credits; use them as a targeted second pass, not a first one.
5. **Instagram / Bluesky** — follow the blogs, engage lightly, then DM or email once the release is live.
6. **Search operators:**
   - `"submit music" ambient blog`
   - `"submit your music" experimental "2025"`
   - `site:submithub.com/blog "ambient"`

## Pitch rules

- One personalized email per target. Mention one specific post/artist that made you think of them.
- Subject line: keep it lowercase and sparse. Example: `new release — photonegative`.
- Include: private streaming link (burnfolder hub), one sentence on the picture, press page, and a clear ask (review, playlist, interview).
- Never attach files unless asked.
- Wait 10–14 days before a polite one-line follow-up.
